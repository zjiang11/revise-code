Abstract.	
  
	
  
Many

degree

   accuracy.

   on	
   difficult

We	
  have	
  given

  conditional	
  independence

  the

  is

  as	
  the	
  best

  using	
  Reproducing	
  Kernel	
  Hilbert

  (RKHS)

  in	
  terms

  The

  is	
  that

  non-­‐Gaussian

  can	
  be	
  scaled

  to	
  much	
  larger	
  problems,	
  both	
  in	
  terms	
  of	
  
greater	
  sample	
  sizes	
  and	
  in	
  terms	
  of	
  larger	
  numbers

  variables.	
  The	
  test	
  can	
  no	
  doubt	
  be

  even	
  faster	
  by	
  implementing	
  it	
  in	
  C++	
  or	
  Fortran,	
  or	
  any	
  lower

  language

  Normal	
  CDF

  is

  in	
  addition	
  to	
  other	
  standard	
  statistical	
  functions.

  are	
  in	
  the	
  process	
  of	
  doing	
  such	
  implementations,

  which	
  case

  test	
  will
