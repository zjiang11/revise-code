A Scheme-Driven Approach to Learning
Programs from Input/Output Equations
Jochen Burghardt
jochen.burghardt alumni.tu-berlin.de
Feb 2018
Abstract
We describe an approach to learn, in a term-rewriting setting, function deﬁnitions
from input/output equations. By conﬁning ourselves to structurally recursive deﬁn-
itions we obtain a fairly fast learning algorithm that often yields deﬁnitions close
to intuitive expectations.
We provide a Prolog prototype implementation of our
approach, and indicate open issues of further investigation.
Key words: inductive functional programming
