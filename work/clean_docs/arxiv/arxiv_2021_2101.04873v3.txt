GLOBAL SMOOTH SOLUTIONS TO THE 3D NON-RESISTIVE MHD
EQUATIONS WITH LOW REGULARITY AXISYMMETRIC DATA
XIAOLIAN AI1 AND ZHOUYU LI2,*
Abstract. The purpose of this paper is to study the incompressible non-resistive MHD
equations in R3. We establish the global well-posedness of the system if the initial data is
axially symmetric and the swirl component of the velocity and the magnetic vorticity vanish.
In particular, the special axially symmetric initial data can be arbitrarily large and satisfy
low regularity assumptions.
Keywords: Non-resistive MHD equations; Axisymmetric solutions; Global regularity
AMS Subject Classiﬁcation: 35Q35, 76D03.
