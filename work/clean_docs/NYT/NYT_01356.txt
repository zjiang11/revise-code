Maya Baratz and Robert F. Jordan were married Feb. 18 by Rabbi Lisa Rubin at the Brooklyn Winery.
Mrs. Jordan, 37, is a visiting fellow at the Nieman Journalism Lab at Harvard, where she focuses on uses of artificial intelligence in storytelling. Until December she was the head of new products for Disney ABC Television in Manhattan, where she developed digital products. She graduated magna cum laude from Wellesley.
She is the daughter of Leor and Haim Baratz of Miami Beach.
Mr. Jordan, 39, works in Manhattan as an analyst and a senior director of business development for Kurtzman Carson Consultants, a Los Angeles firm; he helps restructure companies that are having financial difficulties. He graduated magna cum laude from Rutgers and received a master's degree in international economics, Middle Eastern studies and Arabic language studies from Johns Hopkins. He also received a law degree with honors from George Washington.
Mr. Jordan, who legally changed his name, is a son of Judith S. Feinstein and Bernard Feinstein of Vineland, N.J.
The couple met in Manhattan in 2013 via the dating app Hinge.
