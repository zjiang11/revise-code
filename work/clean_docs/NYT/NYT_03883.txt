Brooke Lauren Fraser, the daughter of Rebecca Sanders Fraser and Gary M. Fraser of Knoxville, Tenn., was married there Saturday to Casey Charles Drummond, a son of Amy Ross Drummond of Marietta, Ga., and John R. Drummond of Milton, Fla. The Rev. Dr. L. Holton Siegling Jr. performed the ceremony at Sequoyah Hills Presbyterian Church, where he is the pastor.
Mrs. Drummond, 24, is a researcher for "NBC Nightly News" in New York. She graduated from the University of Tennessee.
Mr. Drummond, 27, leads the education-industry team in New York for the artificial-intelligence division of IBM. He graduated from Georgia Tech.
The couple met in 2013 at a group of 20-somethings who attended Redeemer Presbyterian Church in New York, where both are now members.
