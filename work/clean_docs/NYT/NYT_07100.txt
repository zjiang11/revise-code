Jaclyn Blair Granick and Seth Robert Flaxman are to be married Sunday at the Park Avenue Club in Florham Park, N.J. Rabbi Daniel Klein is to officiate, with Rabbi Matthew Gewirtz taking part.
The couple, both 25, met at Harvard, from which they graduated, she cum laude and he magna cum laude.
The bride is studying for a Ph.D. in international history and politics at the Graduate Institute of International and Development Studies in Geneva, where she was a Fulbright Scholar in 2008-9 and where she also received a master's in international history and politics.
She is a daughter of Dr. Carol Singer-Granick and Dr. Mark Granick of Livingston, N.J. The bride's father is a professor of surgery at the Newark campus of the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey, where her mother is an associate professor of pediatrics. The bride's father is also the chief and the program director of plastic surgery at the University Hospital in Newark, where the bride's mother serves as the chief of pediatric endocrinology.
The bridegroom is studying for a Ph.D. in machine learning and public policy at Carnegie Mellon University.
He is a son of Judith Flaxman and Kenneth N. Flaxman of Evanston, Ill. His mother is a professor of psychology and the coordinator of the clinical health psychology unit at Argosy University, Chicago, and the director of the Center for Multimodal Treatment, a clinical psychology practice, in Evanston. His father is a lawyer in Chicago.
