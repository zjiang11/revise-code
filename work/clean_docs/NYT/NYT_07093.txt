Rachel Karen Jonas and Robert Andrew Fagnani were married Oct. 1 in West Brighton, Staten Island. Judith N. McMahon, an administrative judge of the Supreme Court of New York, Richmond County Civil Term, officiated at the Snug Harbor Cultural Center and Botanical Garden.
The couple met at Lehigh University, from which they graduated.
The bride, 28, is a neuroscience researcher at the University of California, Los Angeles, from which she received a Ph.D. in neuroscience. She is the daughter of Lynn Mizzy Jonas and Philip D. Jonas of West Brighton.
The groom, 30, leads business development for Takt, a machine-learning software company in San Francisco. He received an M.B.A. from U.C.L.A. He is a son of Mary T. Fagnani and Richard L. Fagnani of Moorestown, N.J.
