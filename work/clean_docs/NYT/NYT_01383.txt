Meghan Elisabeth Healy and Seamus Alan Clancy are to be married Sunday at Harrington Farm in Princeton, Mass., by Kevin Patrick Curran, a friend of the couple, who received permission to officiate from the Commonwealth of Massachusetts.
The bride, 28, will be known as Ms. Healy-Clancy. She is a lecturer in history, literature and social studies at Harvard. She graduated from the University of Chicago and received a master's in history from Harvard, where she also received a doctorate in African studies.
She is the daughter of Judith A. Healy and Daniel M. Healy of Arlington Heights, Ill.
Mr. Clancy, 30, is a computer scientist, specializing in developing artificial-intelligence software, at the Mitre Corporation in Bedford, Mass., which provides research and development services to the federal government. He also graduated from the University of Chicago.
He is the son of Lise M. Clancy and Kevin J. Clancy of Long Valley, N.J.
