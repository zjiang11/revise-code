Hannah Christina Johnsen, the daughter of Cindy K. Currence and Donald P. Johnsen of Phoenix, was married June 3 to Colby James Wise, the son of George O. Wise of Sun City West, Ariz., and the late Gail Perry Wise, and the stepson of Carol Rizzi-Wise of Tempe, Ariz. The Rev. Greg Schlarb, a Roman Catholic priest, performed the ceremony at Mary, Star of the Sea Catholic Church in San Diego. Mrs. Wise, 26, is pursuing a Ph.D. in cancer biology at the Gerstner Sloan Kettering Graduate School of Biomedical Sciences in New York. She graduated from M.I.T.
Mr. Wise, 29, is pursuing a master's degree in computer science specializing in machine learning at Columbia. He graduated magna cum laude from Arizona State. Until 2016, he worked as a trader of agency mortgages at Goldman Sachs in New York.
The couple met in January 2010 during college break on a snowboarding trip to Sunrise Ski Park in Greer, Ariz.
