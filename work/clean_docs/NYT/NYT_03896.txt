Dance
Roslyn Sulcas
There's not much dance in New York this week, but there is plenty to see during the final week of the AMERICAN DANCE FESTIVAL, in Durham, N.C., where the Past/Forward program opens on Monday. It will offer new pieces by Martha Clarke and Bulareyaung Pagarlava, as well as Twyla Tharp's rarely seen "Sweet Fields." Created in 1996, "Fields" is set to 18th-century American hymns by William Billings and Shaker hymns, and Ms. Tharp references Shaker line dances in a piece that evokes the innocence, purity and communal warmth of that tradition. On Thursday the Paul Taylor Dance Company provides a concluding bang for the festival with a new work, "The Uncommitted." Also on that program is one of Mr. Taylor's most acclaimed dances -- a 2002 festival commission -- "Promethean Fire," and his much-loved "Company B." Reynolds Industries Theater, Duke University, and Durham Performing Arts Center; americandancefestival.org.
Back in New York there is the shape-shifting artistry of PILOBOLUS, whose "Seraph," a solo performed with two robots that were created at M.I.T.'s Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory, has its premiere on Monday and will be performed in repertory. Through Aug. 6, Joyce Theater, 175 Eighth Avenue, at 19th Street, (212) 242-0800, joyce.org; $10 to $59.
