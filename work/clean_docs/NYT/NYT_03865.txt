Sarah Rose Wallace and Ian Gary Schwartz are to be married Aug. 6 in Beverly Hills, Calif. Rabbi Jesse M. Olitzky is to officiate at the Beverly Hills Hotel. The bride, 34, is the director of account management in Los Angeles for GumGum, an artificial intelligence company. She graduated from Tulane University.
She is a daughter of Janice B. Wallace and Dr. Daniel J. Wallace of Studio City, Calif. The bride's father is a rheumatologist in Los Angeles and the medical director of the Wallace Rheumatic Study Center in Beverly Hills.
The groom, 33, is a vice president in Los Angeles for the financial services group of the Aon Corporation, a Chicago insurance and financial services company. He graduated from the University of Wisconsin, Madison, and received an M.B.A. from the University of Southern California.
He is the son of Marcia A. Schwartz and Jeffrey B. Schwartz of Monmouth Junction, N.J. The groom's mother retired as a Spanish teacher from J. P. Stevens High School in Edison, N.J. His father is the executive vice president and general counsel in Secaucus, N.J., for Maurice A. Auerbach, a produce distributor. He is also the president of the Jewish Federation in the Heart of New Jersey.
The couple met through a mutual friend in Los Angeles in October 2014.
