Dr. Zoe Danielle Birnbaum and James Matthew Frankel are to be married Feb. 9 by Rabbi Jeffrey Sirkman at Tappan Hill Mansion in Tarrytown, N.Y.
The bride and groom graduated from Colgate.
Dr. Birnbaum, 30, is a third-year resident in the field of psychiatry at NYU Langone Medical Center, and received a medical degree from N.Y.U.
She is a daughter of Dr. Lisa Turtz and Jesse Birnbaum of Larchmont, N.Y. The bride's father is a member of the quality assurance team at the Mahwah, N.J., manufacturing facility of Nobel Biocare, the Swiss-based maker of dental implants and individualized prosthetics. Her mother is a psychiatrist at Montefiore Medical Center in the Bronx, and has a private practice in Larchmont. She is also a painter with a solo exhibition, until March 7, at Barrett Art Center in Poughkeepsie, N.Y.
Mr. Frankel, 29, is a sales productivity manager at Silicon Valley Bank in Manhattan.
He is a son of Julia Frankel and John Frankel of Short Hills, N.J. The groom's mother is a children's book author, who has written several books including four in the "Silly Daddy" series. His father is a founding partner in ff Venture Capital in New York, focusing on fintech, applied artificial intelligence, drones and robotics. He is on the board of overseers of the NYU Tandon School of Engineering in Manhattan
Dr. Birnbaum and Mr. Frankel, who never met while at Colgate, were introduced in 2018 through the dating app Hinge. In May 2019 he proposed outside her maternal grandparents' house in Mamaroneck, N.Y., while fellow alumni from the Colgate 13, an all-male collegiate a cappella group to which he once belonged, sang backup. Later, after she said "yes," Mr. Frankel joined them in a rendition of "My Girl."
