WPP reported a fall in pretax profit for 2023 after booking a large impairment, but said its AI-powered platform WPP Open is rapidly growing its number of customers.
The artificial-intelligence tool is now being used by more than 30,000 people across the group, the company said, adding it continues to invest in AI opportunities, as outlined in its recent capital markets day.
"AI will be fundamental for our business and we are embracing the opportunities that it presents, putting it at the heart of our operations and our work for clients," Chief Executive Mark Read said.
The London advertising group said Thursday that pretax profit was 346.3 million pounds, or $437.6 million, compared with GBP 1.16 billion a year earlier.
Stripping out exceptional and other one-off items headline pretax profit was GBP 1.525 billion compared with GBP 1.60 billion and a consensus of GBP 1.54 billion, taken from FactSet based on eight analysts' forecasts.
WPP booked a goodwill impairment charge of GBP 63.6 million on closed businesses and GBP 727.9 million for the amortization and impairment of acquired intangible assets.
Revenue rose 2.9% to GBP 14.84 billion from GBP 14.43 billion.
Additionally, WPP revenue less pass-through costs was GBP 11.86 billion compared with GBP 11.80 billion a year earlier and in line with a FactSet consensus. Within this, fourth quarter like-for-like revenue less pass-through costs fell 4.5% in the U.S. due to lower spend by technology, healthcare and retail clients.
