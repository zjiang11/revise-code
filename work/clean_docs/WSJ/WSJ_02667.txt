I see parallels between the negative claims about artificial intelligence and the doom-and-gloom predictions regarding Y2K ("Tesla Boss Warns on Artificial Intelligence," Business News, July 17). However, unlike the specific point in time of the year 2000, it will take decades for the ignorance being propagated to be shown as hype and nonsense. Yes, AI and other technologies are doing some amazing things. But the only potential threat would be the level of human dependence on them. There will never be a day when we will be powerless to stop them.
Michael Chiarito
Summerville, S.C.
