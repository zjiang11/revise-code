J.P. Morgan Growth Equity Partners and Index Ventures have led a $35 million investment in Loop, a startup provider of audit and payment systems for logistics companies.
Chicago-based Loop plans to use the funding to invest in research and development and expand beyond ground shipping. It services trucking providers but expects to also develop systems for maritime logistics.
Loop's founders started the business in 2021 while many industries struggled with significant logistical challenges brought on by the Covid-19 pandemic. The startup received $30 million in a Series A investment round last year.
Supply chain disruptions from the pandemic heightened the need for Loop's services, according to Matt McKinney, a co-founder and chief executive. Loop helps logistics businesses digitize shipment data, and uses artificial intelligence and automated audits to cut unnecessary spending.
