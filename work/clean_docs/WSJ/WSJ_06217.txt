Spirit Airlines
Shares of the budget carrier tanked 59% after The Wall Street Journal reported that the airline was preparing to file for bankruptcy, following the breakdown of merger talks with Frontier Airlines. Frontier shares dropped 1.8%, while rival airline JetBlue saw shares rise 9.8%.
