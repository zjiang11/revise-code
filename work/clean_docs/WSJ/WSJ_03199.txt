VENTURE-CAPITAL CONFERENCE AT MASSACHUSETTS INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY
With venture capitalists, entrepreneurs and students on hand, panels will discuss such topics as artificial intelligence and intellectual property. Plus, a "startup showdown" competition. Saturday at MIT Media Lab, Cambridge. Registration starts at $85. mitvcconference.com.
MAKING THE SCI-FI EPIC 'FORBIDDEN PLANET'
Craig Barron and Ben Burtt, Oscar-winning experts in special effects and sound, respectively, look at how the 1956 movie, with Robby the Robot and an unusual electronic score, came to be. Presented by the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences. Saturday at 7 p.m., Smith Rafael Film Center, San Rafael, Calif. oscars.org.
YES, CHEF: MARCUS SAMUELSSON (IN CONVERSATION WITH PAUL HOLDENGRABER)
For Mr. Samuelsson, a childhood of poverty and illness in Africa led to a sojourn in Europe, the Red Rooster restaurant in Harlem and a best-selling 2012 memoir ("Yes, Chef"). He'll appear in the Live From the NYPL series of the New York Public Library's main branch. Monday at 7 p.m., $15-$25. nypl.org.
JEAN-JACQUES ROUSSEAU IN THE ROCKIES
The views of the Swiss philosopher, born 300 years ago, influenced the French Revolution. At this conference on "The Enduring Challenge of Rousseau's Thought," one topic will be "Rousseau Versus Plato on Compassion." Thursday-Saturday, Colorado College, Colorado Springs. rousseauassociation.ish-lyon.cnrs.fr.
