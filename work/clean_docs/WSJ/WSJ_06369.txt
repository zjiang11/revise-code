Samsung Electronics forecast a surge in second-quarter earnings, suggesting that its flagship semiconductor business sustained a strong recovery after a turnaround in the first quarter.
The South Korean technology giant said in a preliminary earnings report Friday that its quarterly operating profit is expected at 10.4 trillion won ($7.52 billion), a nearly 16-fold increase from a year earlier.
The estimate beat a FactSet-compiled consensus forecast of 8.547 trillion won.
Revenue is estimated to have increased 23% to 74 trillion won, Samsung said, above the FactSet consensus estimate.
The company didn't provide further details. It is set to release full quarterly results later this month.
The upbeat guidance suggests that the world's largest memory-chip maker continued its solid earnings recovery, aided by higher prices and brisk demand for powerful computing chips amid the artificial-intelligence boom.
The company's semiconductor business swung to a profit in the first quarter after four straight quarters of losses.
Samsung is striving to regain its edge in the semiconductor business after lagging behind its rivals in advancing high-bandwidth-memory products, a crucial component for AI and other powerful computing devices that are leading a rebound in the chip industry.
Shares were 1.7% higher early Friday, having climbed 3.4% on Thursday before the preliminary earnings release.
