INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS MACHINES
IBM +9.5%
Big Blue is back. IBM on Wednesday said its profit jumped in the latest quarter, thanks to accelerating demand for the legacy tech giant's artificial-intelligence platform, Watsonx. IBM said revenue rose 4% from a year earlier to $17.38 billion, while profit increased more than 20% to $3.29 billion. IBM shares jumped 9.5% to close above $190.43 on Thursday -- the first time IBM stock closed above $190 since June 2013.
UNITED AIRLINES
UAL +5.3%
United Airlines on Tuesday beat analysts' earnings expectations, reporting a $600 million profit for the final three months of 2023. United said it saw durable demand for travel, with growing revenue both from premium and basic economy seats. On Thursday, American Airlines and Southwest Airlines both reported better-than-expected results. Alaska Air Group did as well, but said it had a "challenging start to 2024" after a door plug blew off on a Boeing-made plane on Jan. 5. United shares gained 5.3% Tuesday.
NETFLIX
NFLX +11%
Netflix added about 13 million new subscribers in the latest quarter, roughly 50% more than Wall Street expected. It also struck a new 10-year deal with WWE, getting rights to the show "WWE Raw." The deal, valued at more than $5 billion, comes as Netflix ramps up investment in live programming. Shares of WWE parent TKO Group surged 16% Tuesday, the day the deal was announced. Netflix shares leapt 11% Wednesday.
TESLA
TSLA -12%
Tesla said growth would slow this year and that its profit margin took a hit in the fourth quarter. Tesla faces softening consumer demand, shrinking margins and intensifying competition. Chief Executive Elon Musk also said he wants more control. On Jan. 15, he wrote on the platform X, which he owns, that unless he controls around 25% of Tesla, he would prefer to build artificial intelligence and robotics products elsewhere. Tesla shares sank 12% Thursday.
AMERICAN EXPRESS
AXP +7.1%
Amex card members are still spending, though its quarterly earnings and revenue missed Wall Street expectations. American Express gave a stronger-than-expected outlook for 2024 and said robust consumer spending boosted its results in the latest quarter. The credit-card giant expects its affluent cardholders to continue the trend this year, forecasting full-year revenue growth of 9% to 11%. American Express shares advanced 7.1% Friday.
