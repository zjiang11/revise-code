Japanese smartphone-game provider DeNA Co. said on Thursday it set up a company with Preferred Networks Inc., becoming the latest major firm to bet on the startup's artificial-intelligence technology.
The joint project might mean Nintendo Co.'s future smartphone games would be powered by the technology.
The new company, PFDeNA, will be jointly owned by the two companies and led by DeNA Chief Executive Isao Moriyasu with 30 million yen ($287,100) in capital.
DeNA is most known for its partnership with Japanese videogame powerhouse Nintendo.
