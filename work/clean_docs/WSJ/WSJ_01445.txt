SHANGHAI -- In a sign of its ambition to compete more in artificial intelligence, Tencent Holdings Ltd. said it hired Zhang Tong, a Stanford-trained researcher who formerly led Baidu Inc.'s Big Data Lab, to oversee its fledgling AI program.
Dr. Zhang worked on artificial intelligence at Baidu, as part of his lab-director role there. Baidu suffered a setback in its artificial-intelligence efforts earlier this week when chief scientist Andrew Ng said he was leaving the company.
At Tencent, Dr. Zhang will lead a team of 250 researchers and engineers in a lab set up in April of last year to develop technologies for its gaming, content, social media and cloud businesses. The team will also work closely with AI researchers in Tencent's product groups, the Chinese internet firm said.
Tencent, led by Chairman Pony Ma, is best known for its online games and WeChat social messaging service.
The Shenzhen-based company is expanding its investments in facial recognition, speech recognition and machine translation as it seeks to better monetize its offerings and explore new business areas.
During the company's quarterly earnings report Wednesday, Tencent President Martin Lau told investors that several of the company's content and advertising products would benefit from such software, and said the firm is exploring entering areas such as autonomous driving.
WeChat, a social-messaging platform with more than 889 million users, uses voice-recognition software to help translate different languages into Mandarin text.
