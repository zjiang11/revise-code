Stellantis
Shares in the manufacturer of Chryslers and Jeeps declined 6.3% in U.S. and European trading after Chief Executive Officer Carlos Tavares stepped down.
Intel
The chip manufacturer said Chief Executive Officer Pat Gelsinger had retired and named Chief Financial Officer David Zinsner and Michelle Johnston Holthaus as interim co-CEOs. The company's shares declined 0.5%.
Super Micro Computer
The artificial-intelligence-server maker said it hadn't found evidence that anyone at the company had tried to circumvent export-control regulations, and that it didn't anticipate restating quarterly results for its fiscal 2024 or earlier. The company's shares jumped 29%.
Tesla
The electric-vehicle maker's vice president of AI software said on Saturday that Tesla was rolling out an update for its "Full Self-Driving" driver-assistance software. Shares were up 3.5%.
Novocure and Zai Lab
The companies announced positive trial results for a therapy to treat pancreatic cancer. Novocure shares rocketed 49%. Zai Lab's American depositary receipts rose 1.2%.
XPeng and BYD
The Chinese electric-vehicle makers both reported big year-over-year jumps in deliveries for November. XPeng shares gained 5.3% in the U.S.; BYD increased 2.4% in Hong Kong.
Volkswagen
Thousands of the automaker's workers participated in warning strikes across Germany on Monday as a conflict with management escalated over the company's plans to slash jobs, cut pay and close factories. Shares fell 0.2%.
