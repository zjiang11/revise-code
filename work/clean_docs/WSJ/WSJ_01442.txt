Deutsche Bank Tries
Artificial Intelligence
Deutsche Bank AG, facing a vast body of financial regulations, is turning to artificial intelligence as it sorts through volumes of voice and video recordings to make sure its professionals are complying with the rules.
The move is designed in part to help reduce costs related to regulatory compliance as well as to improve accuracy, when analyzing, say, employees' interactions with clients, said Elly Hardwick, head of innovation at the bank.

U.S. BANCORP
Profits, Lending Rise
U.S. Bancorp said profit and revenue rose in the first quarter as the company reported an increase in loans.
The Minneapolis-based bank, the seventh-largest in the U.S. by assets and the largest regional bank, posted net income of $1.47 billion, up 6.3% from $1.39 billion a year ago. On a per-share basis, earnings rose to 82 cents from 76 cents.
Revenue, a combination of net interest income and fee-based income, climbed 5.7% to $5.32 billion.
-- Joshua Jamerson
