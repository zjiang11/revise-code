Chinese search-engine giant Baidu reported better-than-expected profit as its AI-related business continued to grow, offsetting headwinds from a slowing economy.
The Beijing-based company said Thursday that net profit for the second quarter rose 5.3% from a year earlier to 5.49 billion yuan, equivalent to $769.7 million. That was better than the 5.13 billion yuan expected by analysts in a FactSet poll.
Revenue edged 0.4% lower to 33.93 billion yuan, roughly in line with analysts' estimates of 33.62 billion yuan.
The company said sales from online-marketing services, which make up more than half of overall revenue, fell 2.2%. Baidu's core non-online marketing revenue rose about 2.5%, mainly driven by its AI Cloud business. Revenue from the company's iQIYI video-streaming service fell about 4.65%.
"AI Cloud continued to accelerate in the second quarter, offsetting the ongoing macro headwinds for online marketing revenue and resulting in modestly positive top-line growth for Baidu Core," the company said in a filing.
Baidu has been pursuing new growth drivers in fields such as self-driving cars, cloud computing and artificial intelligence after its core advertising revenue growth slowed in recent years. Its Ernie Bot is one of China's most popular ChatGPT-style bots.
Adjusted net profit, which excludes the effects of share-based compensation expenses, fair-value changes in long-term investments, and disposal gains and losses, fell 7.5% to 7.4 billion yuan. Analysts had expected 6.79 billion yuan, according to FactSet.
