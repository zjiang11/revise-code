Crypto and blockchain-focused venture firm New Form Capital is returning to the fundraising market, seeking to raise a $100 million fund in the latest sign investors remain bullish on the once-beleaguered crypto sector.
New Form says it backs crypto-related technology that aims to reshape traditional finance, including trading markets and payments systems. The New York-based firm also has backed artificial intelligence startups related to crypto.
Venture investors committed $2.4 billion into crypto startups globally in the first quarter, a 40% increase from the prior quarter and an 18% drop from the same quarter a year earlier, according to a report by analytics firm PitchBook Data. While the first quarter's funding was a far cry from the roughly $11 billion crypto startups netted in the first quarter of 2022, the haul snaps seven consecutive quarters of falling investment in their sector.
A rebound in bitcoin, a key underlying asset in the crypto sector, has propelled investor enthusiasm this year. Investors and entrepreneurs said earlier this year that bitcoin's price recovery meant startups were having an easier time tapping venture firms that had been on the fence about deals.
New Form raised a $21 million debut fund in 2020 and closed its second fund, a $65 million vehicle, in 2022. The firm will continue backing technology involving blockchain, financial markets and data. It disclosed its latest fundraising effort in a regulatory filing.
New Form was founded by general partner Alex Marinier, who was previously an investor at DCM Ventures and worked in private equity at Blackstone.
In December, the firm co-led an $11 million Series A funding for crypto finance startup Tres Finance.
