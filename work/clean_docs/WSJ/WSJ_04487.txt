Google parent Alphabet named Eli Lilly Chief Financial Officer Anat Ashkenazi as its next CFO after nearly a yearlong search for a successor to Ruth Porat.
Ashkenazi, who has been Eli Lilly's finance chief since 2021, joins Alphabet as the company invests heavily in artificial intelligence while cutting costs elsewhere, including to shore up resources for its investment push.
Alphabet said last July that Porat, who joined the technology giant nearly a decade ago from Morgan Stanley, would move into a new role as president and chief investment officer, but remain as CFO until a successor was named. The CFO transition takes effect July 31.
Ashkenazi started at Eli Lilly in 2001, and previously served as CFO and controller of the Indianapolis-based drugmaker's Lilly Research Laboratories division. She will be based in the San Francisco Bay Area, and report directly to Chief Executive Sundar Pichai.
"The AI era is giving us an incredible opportunity to innovate at scale across our core products, and to create entirely new products and experiences for our users and customers," Pichai said in a news release announcing Ashkenazi's hiring. "I look forward to working with Anat as we invest responsibly to support our next wave of growth."
Google has stumbled in recent months with the rollout of AI tools and features, for instance saying last week that it would refine its use of AI-powered overviews in search queries after users reported strange and incorrect answers.
