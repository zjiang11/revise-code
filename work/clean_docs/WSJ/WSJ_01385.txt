Luminaries of the technology and media industries converged in Laguna Beach, Calif., for The Wall Street Journal's third WSJDLive conference.
Highlights included AT&T CEO Randall Stephenson and Time Warner CEO Jeff Bewkes discussing their $85 billion deal, Microsoft's Satya Nadella on artificial intelligence, Facebook's Sheryl Sandberg on her company's role in media, and Kobe Bryant on going from National Basketball Association star to venture capitalist -- not to mention interviews with Netflix CEO Reed Hastings, International Business Machines CEO Virginia Rometty, Salesforce.com CEO Marc Benioff, Amazon.com cloud-computing chief Andy Jassy, and many others. This year's gathering also again spotlighted the turbulent world of tech startups and illuminated cutting-edge work in robotics and space exploration.
