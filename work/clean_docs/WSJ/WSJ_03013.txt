Zoom Video Communications will cut almost 2% of staff, the latest in a string of tech-industry layoffs in recent weeks.
The job cuts will be division by division and will comprise less than 2% of Zoom Video's total employee base, a person familiar with the matter said Thursday.
Zoom routinely evaluates its teams "to ensure alignment with our strategy," said a spokesperson for the company.
"As part of this effort, we are rescoping roles to add capabilities and continue to hire in critical areas for the future," the spokesperson said.
The layoffs were earlier reported by Bloomberg.
The person familiar with the matter said the company would continue to add to its head count in 2024, including in areas such as artificial intelligence.
Zoom said a year ago it would cut about 1,300 employees, or around 15% of its staff.
A slate of tech companies have done fresh reductions in the last month. Salesforce, Alphabet-owned Google and Amazon are among the companies that announced layoffs in January.
