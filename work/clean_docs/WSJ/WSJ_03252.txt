Fiat Chrysler Automobiles NV appointed an executive to oversee its North American operations as it seeks to remake itself following the death of its longtime chief executive earlier this year.
Mark Stewart will serve as Fiat Chrysler's chief operating officer for North America, stepping into the role after working most recently at Amazon.com Inc. as vice president of operations, according to an email that Fiat Chief Executive Michael Manley sent employees and released publicly Thursday. Mr. Stewart's job is a newly created stand-alone position, a spokeswoman said.
Mr. Stewart worked at Amazon last year, according to a biography from Fiat Chrysler. There, he supervised customer fulfillment across 200 facilities with 180,000 employees and led teams focused on advanced robotics, artificial intelligence and automation, Mr. Manley wrote.
Fiat appointed Mr. Manley CEO in July after Sergio Marchionne was unable to return to the post following a surgery. Mr. Marchionne, who became CEO of Fiat in 2004, before its merger with Chrysler, died later that month at age 66.
On Thursday, Mr. Manley said Mr. Stewart would also join Fiat Chrysler's executive council. Mr. Stewart has automotive experience, having previously served as operations chief and in numerous other roles at the business now called ZF TRW, a supplier of brakes, steering systems and other automotive components.
The auto maker has also hired Niel Golightly as global chief communications officer, according to Mr. Manley's email. Mr. Golightly's previous job was vice president for external relations in the Americas for Shell Oil Co. Mr. Golightly also will join Fiat Chrysler's executive council.
Additionally, Fiat Chrysler appointed Mike Keegan as chief audit, sustainability and compliance officer. He previously worked as the company's global communications head.
