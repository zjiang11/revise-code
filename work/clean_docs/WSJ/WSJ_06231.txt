Micron Technology
The memory-chip manufacturer reported better-than-expected results and gave guidance that topped expectations. Its stock jumped 15%. Some other major chip players also rose, including Advanced Micro Devices and ASML, which climbed 3.4% and 4.2%, respectively.
Super Micro Computer
The artificial-intelligence server maker is being probed by the Justice Department following a critical report by an activist short-selling firm, according to people familiar with the matter. Shares declined 12%.
Southwest Airlines
The carrier raised its revenue projections and said it will buy back $2.5 billion of stock, as it looks to jump-start profits and fend off an activist investor. The company's stock advanced 5.4%.
Alibaba, Baidu, JD.com, PDD:
U.S.-listed shares of big-name Chinese stocks rallied after China's top leaders pledged stronger support for the economy. Alibaba increased 10%, Baidu rose 9.1%, JD.com climbed 14% and PDD gained nearly 14%.
CarMax
The used-car retailer reported higher quarterly profit, but said its loan business was hit by higher loss provisions. The stock rose 5%.
