Chinese search-engine giant Baidu posted record quarterly revenue and higher adjusted profit, helped by higher ad spending and the rising profile of its ChatGPT-rival chatbot.
The Beijing-based company said Wednesday that revenue in the fourth quarter rose nearly 6% from a year earlier to 34.95 billion yuan, the equivalent of $4.86 billion, as it posted gains in its online marketing and nononline marketing segments. The top line was just below an estimate for 35.1 billion yuan in a FactSet poll of analysts.
Net profit fell 48% to 2.6 billion yuan, missing estimates for 4.96 billion yuan in a FactSet poll, weighed by losses related to an equity method investment, Baidu said. Operating profit rose 17%, while non-GAAP income, which excludes share-based compensation expenses and fair-value changes to investments, among others, climbed 44% to 7.76 billion yuan.
