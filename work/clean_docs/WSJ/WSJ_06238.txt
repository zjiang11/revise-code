Nvidia
The AI chip maker's stock, which has more than doubled this year, fell 2.1% ahead of its earnings report after the closing bell. Salesforce, CrowdStrike and HP, which also reported after the bell, all declined around 2%.
Super Micro Computer
Shares of the AI server maker slid 19% after it delayed it annual filing with the Securities and Exchange Commission, extending losses spurred by a short-seller report Tuesday.
Nordstrom and Kohl's
The department-store chains both lifted annual guidance after posting strong quarterly results. Kohl's shares fell 0.3% while Nordstrom rose 4.2%.
Foot Locker
The sportswear retailer's quarterly results topped expectations but it kept full-year guidance unchanged and announced store closures in Asia and Europe. The stock dropped 10%.
Box
The cloud-storage company raised its annual outlook and said its artificial-intelligence offering was helping drive growth. Shares advanced 11%.
PVH
The Calvin Klein parent reported a drop in quarterly sales and said consumer weakness in China and Australia was weighing. Shares declined 6.4%.
J.M. Smucker
The food maker cut full-year guidance, citing cautious spending from inflation-weary shoppers. The stock closed down 5%.
