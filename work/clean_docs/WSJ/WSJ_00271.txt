Throughout his presidency and 2020 re-election bid, Donald Trump was known to keep a rotating cast of advisers, from campaign officials to political operatives and his children.
However in 2024, the former president's inner circle -- whether members of the campaign or outside advisers -- has remained small and stable, and his campaign operations are more disciplined than in years past.
On the first day of the Republican National Convention, Trump officially named Sen. J.D. Vance of Ohio as his running mate for his second presidential campaign.
Many of Trump's closest advisers have been his inner circle since 2016, having previously worked as political consultants, pollsters or in some capacity to the Republican Party before joining Trump world.
Inside the campaign
-- Steven Cheung has served on all of Trump's campaigns and in his administration. He was named assistant communications director at the White House and was also part of the team who helped with the appointment of Supreme Court Justice Neil Gorsuch.
-- Tony Fabrizio rejoined his current campaign earlier this year. He previously worked as top strategist for a pro-Trump super PAC and as part of the 2016 campaign.
-- Boris Epshteyn is a senior adviser to the Trump campaign. He is among few current staffers to have worked for Trump, on and off, since 2016. Epshteyn helped to craft Trump's public legal strategy and was often seen in the courtroom during his hush-money trial.
-- Jason Miller is a senior adviser with Trump's 2024 campaign and is among few current campaign staffers to have worked for Trump, on and off, since he first ran for office in 2016.
-- Dan Scavino is among the longest-serving Trump aides in the White House and is one of Trump's top advisers. His role expanded from social media to broader communications while at the White House, as well as for the 2024 campaign.
-- Vince Haley and Ross Worthington are speech writers who also craft Trump's policy statements. Both previously worked under Stephen Miller at the Trump White House.
As part of his inner campaign circle, Trump has added three new advisers.
-- Susie Wiles is a senior campaign adviser who quickly grew in influence and is often considered his de facto campaign manager and credited with imposing a more professional operation in comparison to his previous White House bids.
-- Chris LaCivita is one of the most influential officials in Trump's inner circle. He joined Trump's 2024 campaign as a senior adviser and previously headed two pro-Trump super PACs. LaCivita assumed a senior role with the Republican National Committee, helping to tighten Trump's grip on the party.
-- Brian Hughes is a veteran Florida-based operative and is close to Wiles. He helps oversee interactions with reporters.
Outside the campaign
Trump also has close confidants who aren't part of his latest election bid but continue to advise him and members of his campaign on policy issues.
Several of his outside advisers were also part of his 2016 campaign.
-- Corey Lewandowski was Trump's first campaign manager and has remained in the former president's inner circle. Most recently he has worked as an outside consultant to the campaign via the Republican National Committee.
-- Kellyanne Conway served as Trump's counsel at the White House, as well as his third campaign manager. She frequently appeared on television, offering fierce defense of his policies and record.
-- Stephen Miller is among few current campaign staffers to have worked for Trump, on and off, since he first ran for office in 2016.
-- Kash Patel, one of Trump's closest advisers and supporters, has served in various roles during his presidency. He is known for pushing conspiracy theories on social media that echo many of Trump's own claims about stolen elections, Covid vaccines and more.
-- Ric Grenell served in several roles during the Trump administration, including Trump's ambassador to Germany, special envoy for Serbia and Kosovo, and acting director of national intelligence. More recently, he has worked as an outside consultant for an investment firm founded by Jared Kushner. A cancer survivor, he helped develop an artificial intelligence technology for chronic cancer patients.
-- Justin Caporale is Trump's current deputy campaign manager for operations.
-- Larry Kudlow, the Fox Business Network anchor and veteran policy adviser, served as the top White House economic adviser for part of Trump's presidency and remains a close confidant.
-- Sean Hannity is a Fox News anchor who has maintained a close relationship with Trump, despite Trump's on-and-off frustration with Fox News' coverage of him.
In the family
Among Trump's immediate family, only his two eldest sons, Donald Jr. and Eric, have maintained a visible presence on the campaign trail, often stumping for their father at rallies, joining him in the courtroom and advocating on his behalf in the media. His daughter Tiffany Trump appeared in court to support her father during his New York hush-money trial and was among the delegates at the convention officially nominating Trump for the Republican ticket.
His daughter Ivanka and her husband, Jared Kushner, both served in senior roles in the Trump White House, but have remained private citizens in this election cycle. Both have said they have no intention of joining a future Trump administration.
