Zoom Video Communications shares gained after the video-conferencing provider forecast higher-than-expected earnings this year.
Zoom's stock climbed 8% on Tuesday.
The company said on Monday that it expects an adjusted per-share profit of $4.85 to $4.88 for the current fiscal year. Analysts polled by FactSet had expected an adjusted per-share profit of $4.66.
Zoom said this month that it would cut nearly 2% of the company's staff, becoming the latest technology company to reduce its workforce.
Zoom said rising profitability and a boost to sales from artificial-intelligence services helped the company's quarterly results.
Revenue rose 2.6% to $1.15 billion, slightly ahead of consensus forecasts.
Stripping out certain one-time items, the company's adjusted per-share profit was $1.42, ahead of the $1.15 forecast by analysts, according to FactSet.
Enterprise customers were up 3.5% from a year earlier, at 220,400.
As for the company's outlook, Zoom expects about $4.6 billion in revenue in fiscal 2025, compared with the $4.64 billion expected by analysts.
