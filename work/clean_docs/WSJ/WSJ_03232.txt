ConcertAI LLC has raised $150 million to expand a business that uses software and data to help speed clinical research into different types of cancer and to match patients with clinical trials and medications.
New investor Sixth Street Partners provided the Series C financing to Cambridge, Mass.-based ConcertAI, which said it raised this round at a $1.9 billion valuation. ConcertAI has now raised a total of $330 million in venture capital since forming in 2017, according to Chief Executive Jeff Elton.
Other current investors in ConcertAI include Declaration Partners, Maverick Ventures, AllianceBernstein and SymphonyAI, Dr. Elton said.
ConcertAI's funding is part of a wave of venture investment in startups that use software to make clinical trials more efficient and accessible to more patients.
According to Dr. Elton, the vision of ConcertAI is to use artificial intelligence to help drug companies and doctors make better use of data.
"The concept was to look for an industry that is data-rich and insight-light," he said.
Electronic medical records, for example, include valuable information in the form of unstructured data such as doctors' notes or the contents of PDFs, Dr. Elton said. Using natural-language processing, ConcertAI can convert that information into a form that can be read and analyzed by a machine. Through AI, the company can then glean insights.
Doctors can use the technology to identify patients who are eligible for a clinical trial, based on the person's medical history and lab data, Dr. Elton said. ConcertAI has information on clinical trials available within and outside the patient's hospital, according to Dr. Elton.
