IBM
Wager on AI Sends
Profit Above Views
International Business Machines posted results that topped Wall Street expectations, aided by a bet on AI.
The tech company's book of business for Watsonx has topped $2 billion, after the generative-AI platform launched last year. About one-quarter of that relates to software solutions, and the rest from consulting.
Overall revenue rose about 2%, led by a 7% increase in software revenue. Infrastructure revenue climbed 1%, while consulting revenue dropped at a similar rate.
IBM raised its outlook for free cash flow, which it now expects to top $12 billion this year. Revenue came in at $15.77 billion, ahead of Wall Street expectations of $15.62 billion, according to FactSet. Profit rose to $1.83 billion from $1.58 billion a year earlier.

ROYAL CARIBBEAN
Earnings Surge on
Strong Demand
Royal Caribbean nearly doubled its quarterly profit, with higher booking volumes at record pricing.
The Miami-based company's revenue exceeded analysts' expectations.
Strong demand for European and Alaskan cruises is expected to drive growth this quarter. For the full year, Royal Caribbean expects adjusted earnings to grow 68% to a range of $11.35 to $11.45 a share.
Profit of $854 million, or $3.11 a share, compared with $459 million, or $1.70 a share, a year earlier.
Stripping out one-time items, adjusted earnings per share were $3.21. Analysts polled by FactSet had expected $2.76.
Revenue rose to $4.11 billion, exceeding the $4.05 billion expected by analysts.

HARLEY-DAVIDSON
Motorcycle Maker
Reduces Forecast
Harley-Davidson said quarterly sales and earnings came in well above analyst expectations, though retail sales fell and the company's profit forecast darkened.
The Milwaukee-based motorcycle maker reported quarterly earnings per share of $1.63 and $1.6 billion in revenue. That compared with the $1.40 and $1.3 billion predicted by analysts, according to FactSet.
Harley sold 50,000 motorcycles worldwide during the three months ended June 30, a 3% decline from 2023.
Harley lowered its full-year forecast, saying motorcycle revenue will likely drop 5% to 9%, with an operating income margin of 10.6% to 11.6%. It earlier predicted revenue would be flat to down 9%, with margins of 12.6% to 13.6%.

NYCB
Regional Bank
Posts Net Loss
New York Community Bancorp shares dropped about 10% after the regional bank disclosed another quarterly net loss and girded for further trouble in commercial real estate lending.
NYCB logged a second-quarter loss of $323 million, compared with net income of more than $400 million a year earlier.
The Hicksville, N.Y., bank said more loans in commercial real estate had gone bad and that it had added to its reserves for potential future losses. NYCB expanded a loan review to cover 75% of commercial real estate and multi-family portfolios, it said.
The bank fell into turmoil early this year due to distress in those books, particularly for loans backed by rent-stabilized multifamily properties in New York City.
-- Gina Heeb
