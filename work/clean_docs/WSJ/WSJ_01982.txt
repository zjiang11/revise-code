There is a difference between artificial intelligence and earlier industrial revolutions, when human beings were sitting at the controls, commanding the machines ("Smart Machines Will Free Us All," Review, April 15). The AI revolution is unlike the historical industrial revolution in that it reverses this relationship: It puts the machine at the controls to command the humans. When I entered the computer industry 35 years ago, my mentor was fond of saying that ultimately the only question about computers that matters is whether the machines will want pets. I scoffed at him then. I am not scoffing now.
Frederick Butzen
Chicago
