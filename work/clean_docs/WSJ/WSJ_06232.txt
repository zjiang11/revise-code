Micron Technology stock jumped after the memory-chip maker posted better-than-expected quarterly results and issued guidance that topped Wall Street expectations.
The stock climbed nearly 15% on Thursday, logging its best one-day percentage gain since 2011.
The move helped buoy the broader tech sector, with the Nasdaq Composite gaining less than 0.6%.
Micron shares had closed Wednesday at $95.77 each, up about 12% since the beginning of the year.
The company said robust artificial-intelligence-related demand helped sales of chips for data centers. Overall revenue nearly doubled from a year earlier.
Micron expects the momentum to continue in the current quarter, saying both revenue and adjusted earnings are likely to beat analyst views.
Revenue for the latest quarter came in at $7.75 billion, beating Wall Street expectations by about 1.3%, according to FactSet.
Profit rose to $887 million, compared with a loss of $1.43 billion in the same period a year earlier.
Adjusted earnings of $1.18 a share topped analysts expectations by about 6%.
Micron expects fiscal first-quarter revenue between $8.5 billion and $8.9 billion and adjusted per-share earnings between $1.66 and $1.82. Both are ahead of analyst estimates.
