ENTITLEMENTS DEBATE: NICHOLAS EBERSTADT, WILLIAM GALSTON
Are legislated benefits corrupting or a civic responsibility? The authors debated this issue in articles in the Journal's Review section last month; now they'll speak at New York's New School for Social Research. Thursday at 6 p.m., John L. Tishman Auditorium. Free. WSJ Live stream. newschool.edu/cps/events.
THE SINGULARITY SUMMIT 2012 IN SAN FRANCISCO
The merging of humankind and artificial intelligence ("the singularity") is closer than you think, proponents say. Speakers include cognitive scientist Steven Pinker, Nobelist Daniel Kahneman and Google Director of Research Peter Norvig. Saturday-Sunday at Nob Hill Masonic Center. Two-day pass $795. singularitysummit.com.
CIVIL LIBERTIES DEBATE ON FIVE UNIVERSITY CAMPUSES
From Monday to Friday, Glenn Greenwald (liberal), Bruce Fein (conservative) and Jacob Hornberger (libertarian) will stop at the University of Washington; the University of California, Davis; U.C. San Diego; the University of Arizona; and the University of Colorado, Boulder. Free. yaliberty.org/tour.

Wednesday at 7:30 p.m., Peter Jay Sharp Theatre at Symphony Space. Tickets $15-$28. symphonyspace.org.
