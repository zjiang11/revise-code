Email security company Abnormal Security Corp. has raised $210 million in a Series C funding round led by Insight Partners.
Based in San Francisco, Abnormal uses machine learning and other forms of artificial intelligence to detect potential cyberattacks launched through cloud-based email systems such as Microsoft Corp.'s Office 365 or Alphabet Inc.'s Gmail. These include targeted and social-engineering attacks and business email compromise attempts. They also include supply-chain infiltrations, where hacker emails might not include typical nefarious elements, such as malware attachments or malicious links.
The company's funding round also saw participation from venture capital investors Greylock Partners and Menlo Ventures.
