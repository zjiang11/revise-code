Bret Taylor, the former co-chief executive of Salesforce Inc., is launching an artificial-intelligence startup with a longtime veteran of Google, becoming the latest big name to dive into the race to try to cash in on AI.
Mr. Taylor, who has backed multiple startups and worked with some of the highest-profile leaders in technology, said the company will try to ride what some see as the next wave of innovation.
"Rarely do you encounter a new technology so powerful that it feels inevitable that it will change the course of every industry," Mr. Taylor said in a LinkedIn post.
He will be joining forces with Clay Bavor, who will be leaving his job as vice president of labs at Alphabet Inc.'s Google. In a LinkedIn post, Mr. Bavor said the new company will develop solutions using AI.
Mr. Taylor, who just finished a stint as co-CEO of Salesforce, said via email that the two wouldn't yet be announcing information about the startup beyond the LinkedIn posts.
"We are just getting started," he said.
Mr. Taylor served as co-CEO of Salesforce with Marc Benioff for about a year. He had joined the company in 2016 after it acquired his startup Quip Inc.
Mr. Taylor also served as chairman of Twitter Inc. for about a year before Elon Musk bought the company in a deal valued at $44 billion in October.
Messrs. Taylor and Bavor briefly overlapped at Google at the start of their careers in 2005, according to their LinkedIn profiles.
